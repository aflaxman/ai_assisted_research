# Tables transcribed from the paper's supplementary appendix

Source: Dhungel et al. (2025), J Nutr 155:4486-4501, supplementary data
(mmc1.pdf, 28 pages), parsed from the PDF text with a regular expression
and checked by eye against the page images.

- `table_s1_sodium_intake.csv`: mean and SD of sodium intake (g/day) by sex,
  five-year age group and SEIFA quintile (1 = most deprived), from Trieu et al.
  2021. Ages under 25 repeat the 25 to 29 row; 85 to 89 is the last row.
- `table_s2_sbp.csv`: mean and SD of systolic blood pressure (mmHg) by the same
  strata, from the 2017/18 National Health Survey regression; 110 (SD 10)
  under 25.
- `table_s3_sbp_equation.csv`, `table_s4_covariance.csv`: the sodium-to-SBP
  meta-regression coefficients and their covariance.
