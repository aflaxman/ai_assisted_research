## 1. AIPAC's Electoral Apparatus and 2024 Baseline

**The apparatus.** AIPAC operates through: (a) **AIPAC PAC**, a hybrid conventional PAC
that endorses candidates and bundles/earmarks member contributions; (b) **United
Democracy Project (UDP)**, its super PAC (founded January 2022, same month AIPAC first
donated directly to federal candidates — per [Sludge, Aug 3, 2026](https://readsludge.com/2026/08/03/aipac-tops-100-million-in-election-spending-for-second-straight-cycle/));
and (c) allied **DMFI PAC** (Democratic Majority for Israel).

**2024 cycle totals** (per [Sludge's FEC analysis, Jan 24, 2025](https://readsludge.com/2025/01/24/here-is-all-the-money-aipac-spent-on-the-2024-elections/)):

- Combined AIPAC PAC + UDP spending: **~$126.9 million** ($100M+ is the widely cited
  round figure — [Common Dreams](https://www.commondreams.org/news/aipac-100-million),
  [New Republic](https://newrepublic.com/post/190021/report-aipac-spent-record-amount-2024-election))
- Direct/bundled candidate contributions: **$55.2M+** (at least $45.2M to members of the 119th Congress)
- UDP disbursements: **~$61M**, of which **~$37.9M** were House-race independent
  expenditures and ~$8.6M contributions to other PACs; funded by seven-figure checks
  from billionaire donors. See also [The Intercept's dollar tracker, Oct 24, 2024](https://theintercept.com/2024/10/24/aipac-spending-congress-elections-israel/).

**2024 marquee races:**

- **NY-16 (June 25, 2024):** George Latimer (recruited by AIPAC) defeated Rep. Jamaal
  Bowman **58.4%–41.6%**. Total ad spending ~$25M (AdImpact), then the most expensive
  House primary ever; **~$15M from UDP**
  ([NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/2024-election/jamaal-bowman-george-latimer-ny-house-primary-results-rcna158487),
  [ABC/538](https://abcnews.com/538/pro-israel-groups-spent-big-oust-squad-members/story?id=113675889)).
- **MO-01 (Aug 6, 2024):** Wesley Bell defeated Rep. Cori Bush ~51%–46%; **UDP spent
  ~$8.5–9M** ([Axios](https://www.axios.com/2024/08/04/cori-bush-wesley-bell-aipac-negative-ads),
  [JTA 2026 retrospective](https://www.jta.org/2026/08/03/default/cori-bush-promised-to-bury-aipac-her-rematch-with-wesley-bell-in-missouri-tests-that)
  cites "$9 million influx"). Combined, Bowman and Bush were unseated by candidates
  backed by **$29M+ in AIPAC-linked money**.
- (Background, not re-verified today: UDP declined to spend against Summer Lee (PA-12)
  and Ilhan Omar (MN-05) in 2024, both of whom survived — a "selection into treatment"
  caveat for any causal analysis.)

## 2. AIPAC's 2026 Involvement: Totals, Tactics, Race-by-Race

- **$104.3M+ combined AIPAC PAC + UDP spending** for the 2026 cycle — second consecutive
  $100M+ cycle; UDP still had **~$80M on hand** at end of June
  ([Sludge, Aug 3, 2026](https://readsludge.com/2026/08/03/aipac-tops-100-million-in-election-spending-for-second-straight-cycle/);

  spending through pop-up PACs that delay donor disclosure until after Election Day

  **BOLD America** ($650K from UDP, pro-Espaillat NY-13;
  [City & State NY](https://www.cityandstateny.com/politics/2026/06/aipac-helping-boost-espaillat-against-dsa-challenge/414300/));

## 4. Public Opinion Data (2024–2026)

- **Data for Progress / Drop Site / Zeteo, Michigan (Apr 2–8, 2026; n=515 likely Dem
  primary voters):** **64% less likely** to support a Senate candidate taking
  AIPAC/pro-Israel group donations vs **10% more likely**; **62% agreed** a candidate who
  won't stand up to AIPAC won't stand up for Michiganders (13% disagreed). Horse race
  then: Stevens 23, El-Sayed 22, McMorrow 22
  ([Zeteo](https://zeteo.com/p/exclusive-poll-michigans-dem-senate),
  [Drop Site](https://www.dropsitenews.com/p/michigan-senate-democratic-primary-poll-aipac)
  — Drop Site page 403'd; numbers via search index and
  [Common Dreams](https://www.commondreams.org/news/michigan-senate-primary)).
- **Data for Progress conjoint survey experiment (July 17–19, 2026; n=1,207 US likely
  voters; 7 forced-choice D-vs-R matchups; 11 randomized funding sources; WLS with
  demographic controls, clustered SEs):** Democrats funded by AIPAC, AI, crypto, or
  pharma **significantly underperform** a Democrat who "rejects all financial support
  from outside industry groups and PACs"; AIPAC funding also performs significantly worse
  than Planned Parenthood or nurses-union funding; one reported comparison: **6.2 points
  worse against a Republican**
  ([DFP blog, July 28, 2026](https://www.dataforprogress.org/blog/2026/7/28/aipac-ai-and-crypto-funded-democrats-underperform-democrats-who-reject-corporate-pac-money)).

  **not their top issue** despite the attention
