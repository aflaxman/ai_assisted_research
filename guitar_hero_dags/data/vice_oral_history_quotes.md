# Source quotes: demo stations and Guitar Hero sales

All quotes verbatim from: Blake Hester, "The Oral History of 'Guitar Hero',"
VICE (Waypoint), January 27, 2021.
<https://www.vice.com/en/article/the-oral-history-of-guitar-hero/>

## Best Buy: the first demos (case reports)

> **Charles Huang (co-founder and vice president of business, RedOctane):**
> They were the first to kind of figure out that demos were gonna sell the
> product. I don't know if it came from the corporate level or the store
> manager level, but we started seeing demo stations set up at Best Buy. At
> the time, they didn't have a dedicated demo station in the game aisle,
> they took it and walked over to the TV set aisle and set up a demo in
> front of the TV. We started seeing these pop up in Best Buy and they did
> really well with that.

## Walmart: the scanner data (retrospective cohort)

> **Charles Huang:** Walmart were really the first to give us actual data.
> In their software data for stores, they started noticing, like, "Hey,
> there are some stores selling 18 units a week and everybody else is
> selling two. What's going on there?" They went and looked, and they said,
> "Oh, the ones that are selling 18, the store manager had set up a demo."
> They decided, "We're gonna do demos across all of our stores." But that
> was really pioneered by Best Buy and then Walmart kind of, like, dug into
> the data and figured that out. Once they gave us that data we told all
> the retailers, "Hey, this is the difference." That became probably the
> biggest marketing tool for Guitar Hero, was just the in store demos.

## GameStop: the mailed demo kits (encouragement design)

> **Corey Fong (senior brand manager, RedOctane):** I sent an email to the
> buyer, you know, "Can we get permission to get demo stations in all of
> your kiosks at your stores?" He said, flat out, "No." [...] At that point
> when he said no, it's like, "OK, forget that, I'm going to go around
> you." [...] So we go to the GameStop Managers [Expo] [...] "We're taking
> names and addresses of stores. Our whole purpose here is to just capture
> names on [an] Excel sheet, and when we get back we're going to have to
> mail it out physically ourselves." [...] we started sending demo discs
> out, demo discs along with controllers. [...]
>
> I think it was maybe three weeks later? I get an email from the same
> buyer that said no to me about getting demo stations set up. He's all
> like, "Remember when we said no? I want demo stations in our stores now."
> I asked him "what changed your mind?" And he says, "Well, I noticed one
> store's getting freaking 10 pre-orders a week and this other one's only
> getting two. And so I went to go call up my managers at those stores,
> like, "What are you doing? Got a demo station set up."

## The guerrilla arm (exposure was never random)

> **Lennon Lange (associate producer, RedOctane):** One of the things that
> I did without the company knowing [...] I would drive all over the Bay
> Area with a TV, a test kit, and the game in my car. I would set up shop
> at like Guitar Centers, GameStops, anywhere I could that would let me set
> up to demo the game and to get in people's hands. [...] It started a
> groundswell, from what I saw.

## Extracted numbers

| Retailer | With demo | Without demo | Ratio | How the contrast arose |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Walmart | 18 units/wk | 2 units/wk | 9.0 | Corporate noticed outlier stores in scanner data, investigated, found manager-installed demos |
| GameStop | 10 pre-orders/wk | 2 pre-orders/wk | 5.0 | Buyer noticed after RedOctane mailed demo kits to self-selected managers |
